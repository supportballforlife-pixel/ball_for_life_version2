01 / SHIPPING

Delivery Times

At Ball For Life, we aim to get your order to you as quickly as possible.

Most orders are expected to arrive within 4–7 working days after dispatch. Delivery times are estimates and can vary depending on your location, courier delays, customs processing and other circumstances outside of our control.

For some international or overseas shipments, delivery may take up to 3 weeks. Longer delivery times can occur due to overseas transit, customs or courier disruption.

02 / DELIVERY DELAYS

Parcel Not Arrived?

If you have not received your parcel within 3 weeks of dispatch, please contact us as soon as possible with your order number so that we can investigate the shipment.

Claims regarding parcels that have not arrived should be raised promptly. If you do not contact us within 2 months of the expected delivery date, our ability to investigate the shipment with the courier may be limited and we may be unable to assist with a delayed-delivery claim.

Lost / Undelivered Parcels

Where an order is confirmed as lost or undelivered, Ball For Life will provide an appropriate resolution, which may include a replacement or full refund, subject to your applicable consumer rights.

03 / 14-DAY CANCELLATION

Change-Of-Mind Returns

If you buy from us online as a UK consumer, you normally have the right to cancel your order within 14 days after the day you receive it. You do not need to give a reason.

After telling us that you want to cancel, you should send the item back within 14 days. Please contact us first so we can confirm the return address and the details we need to match the return to your order.

This right may not apply to items that have been personalised, made to your specification, or sealed items that are not suitable for return for hygiene reasons once unsealed.

Good To Know

Please try items on indoors only and keep all packaging until you know you are keeping the order.

04 / FAULTY OR INCORRECT PRODUCTS

Faults, Damage Or Wrong Items

Please inspect your order as soon as it arrives. Contact us promptly if the item is faulty, damaged, not as described, or if we sent the wrong product, colour or size.

  • Include your order number.
  • Send clear photographs or videos showing the issue.
  • Keep the item and packaging while we review the claim.

If the issue is confirmed, we will offer a suitable resolution, which may include a replacement, repair, refund, or return instructions depending on the circumstances and your consumer rights.

05 / RETURN CONDITION

Condition Requirements

For change-of-mind returns, items should be returned in a resaleable condition: unworn, unwashed, unused, free from marks, odours or damage, and with any original tags or packaging included where possible.

You can inspect and try on clothing as you would in a shop, but please take reasonable care of it. If an item is returned used, damaged, heavily marked, or missing parts because it was handled more than necessary, we may make a deduction from the refund where allowed by law.

06 / RETURN POSTAGE

Who Pays Return Postage?

For change-of-mind cancellations, you are responsible for the cost of returning the item unless we tell you otherwise.

If the item is faulty, damaged, incorrect or not as described, we will explain the return postage process when we review your claim.

We recommend using a tracked service and keeping proof of postage until your return has been processed.

07 / REFUNDS

Refund Processing Time

Once your return is received and checked, approved refunds will usually be processed within 14 days back to the original payment method.

Your bank or payment provider may take extra time to show the money in your account after we process it.

Original delivery charges are handled in line with your consumer rights and the reason for the return.
