01 / SHIPPING

Delivery Times

At Ball For Life, we aim to get your order to you as quickly as possible.

Most orders are expected to arrive within 4–7 working days after dispatch. Delivery times are estimates and can vary depending on your location, courier delays, customs processing and other circumstances outside of our control.

For some international or overseas shipments, delivery may take up to 3 weeks. Longer delivery times can occur due to overseas transit, customs or courier disruption.

02 / DELIVERY DELAYS

Parcel Not Arrived?

If you have not received your parcel within 3 weeks of dispatch, please contact us as soon as possible with your order number so that we can investigate the shipment.

Claims regarding parcels that have not arrived should be raised promptly. If you do not contact us within 2 months of the expected delivery date, our ability to investigate the shipment with the courier may be limited and we may be unable to assist with a delayed-delivery claim.

Lost / Undelivered Parcels

Where an order is confirmed as lost or undelivered, Ball For Life will provide an appropriate resolution, which may include a replacement or full refund, subject to your applicable consumer rights.

03 / RETURNS

Return Eligibility

Please inspect your order as soon as it arrives.

Under our store returns policy, a return request may be considered where the item you received is:

  • Seriously damaged or defective when received.
  • A different product from the one you ordered.
  • The wrong colour compared with your confirmed order.
  • The wrong size compared with your confirmed order.

Please contact us within 7 days of receiving your order if one of the above applies. Return requests made after this period may not be accepted under our store policy.

When contacting us, please provide your order number and clear photographs or videos showing the issue. Items should remain unworn and, where reasonably possible, in their original condition while your claim is being reviewed.

Returns requested under this store policy for reasons outside the circumstances listed above may not be accepted. Nothing in this policy limits any cancellation, refund, return, repair, replacement or other rights you may have under applicable consumer law.

04 / DAMAGED ITEMS

What Counts As Damage?

We will review the condition of the product and the evidence you provide before approving a damaged-item return under this store policy.

Examples of potentially serious damage may include substantial tears, major holes, broken or unusable components, significant printing defects, severe staining or other faults that materially affect the product's appearance or use.

Minor variations, loose threads, packaging marks or other small imperfections that do not materially affect the product may not be considered serious damage.

Assessment

Ball For Life will assess the evidence provided when determining whether an item qualifies as damaged or defective under this store policy. This assessment does not affect your statutory rights where an item is faulty, damaged or not as described.

05 / INCORRECT ORDERS

Wrong Item, Colour Or Size

If we have sent you the wrong product, colour or size, contact us within 7 days of delivery and include your order number together with clear photographs of the item received.

Once the issue has been reviewed and confirmed, we will provide instructions for resolving the order.

Contacting Us

For shipping or return enquiries, please provide as much information as possible so that we can review your case quickly.
