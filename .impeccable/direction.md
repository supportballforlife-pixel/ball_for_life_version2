# Ball For Life Store Redesign

THESIS: Basketball culture becomes an independent fashion store with clear, restrained shopping controls.

OWN-WORLD: The user's pinned Nike/Represent-inspired editorial direction. Black #111111, white #ffffff, off-white #f5f4f0, charcoal #252525. Barlow Condensed display lettering and Inter for shopping controls. Real campaign, product and customer media provide the visual character.

STORY: Campaign hero -> four newest tees -> brand campaign -> real customer and creator proof -> category destinations -> newsletter -> support footer. Product pages show a large gallery beside size, colour, quantity and add controls. Customer-care pages emphasise readable real policy content. Admin pages remain compact and functional.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim shipping bar, logo-left navigation on desktop and centred logo on mobile, then an unframed full-width photograph. BALL FOR LIFE is the prominent headline. The Shop The Drop action sits beside the real model photograph. The next New Arrivals section is visible at the bottom of a common phone/laptop viewport.

FORM: Code-led implementation of the user's explicitly pinned direction. Impeccable's concept-seed command was attempted and failed because its downloadable engine was unavailable. Existing brand assets and product truth govern the replacement. No generated comps or invented claims.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Natural touch scrolling through customer media, with manual photo slides and visible video playback controls. One 20px, 700ms story reveal; motion is disabled when the user requests reduced motion. Product front/back previews and selection feedback use approximately 200ms transitions.

VALIDATION: Capture homepage, collection, product, checkout, login, story and support pages on desktop and mobile. Check layouts at 375, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440px. Validate search, colour/size selection, quick add, cart quantities, checkout totals, currency, coming-soon states and reduced motion. Live payment and email services must not be invoked by local checks.
