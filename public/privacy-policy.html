Privacy Policy

Ball For Life respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we use it, which service providers help us operate the store, and the choices and rights available to you.

Last updated: 14 October 2026
Who We Are Information We Collect How We Use It Payments Marketing Service Providers Data Retention Your Rights Security Contact
01 / WHO WE ARE

Ball For Life

Ball For Life is a UK-based sole trader business operating the Ball For Life online store.

For privacy questions or requests relating to your personal information, you can contact us at supportballforlife@gmail.com.

We do not publish our home or trading address on this privacy page. Where the law requires us to provide additional business information to a customer or authority, we will do so through the appropriate channel.

02 / INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Personal Information

The information we collect depends on how you use the website. This can include:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your delivery address and other delivery details.
  • Account login and account-related information.
  • Order history and products purchased.
  • Information you provide when contacting customer support.
  • Marketing subscription information where you choose to join our mailing list.
  • Technical information needed to operate and secure the website, such as basic browser, device or network information where collected by our hosting or security providers.
Important

Creating an account, placing an order or providing your email address for delivery and order purposes does not automatically subscribe you to marketing emails.

03 / HOW WE USE INFORMATION

Why We Need It

We may use your personal information to:

  • Create and manage your Ball For Life account.
  • Process and fulfil your orders.
  • Store the delivery information needed to send your parcel.
  • Communicate with you about your order, payment, delivery or account.
  • Respond to support requests, returns, delivery issues or disputes.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse or security problems.
  • Keep business and transaction records where required by law.
  • Send marketing messages where you have separately chosen to subscribe.

Depending on the situation, we may process information because it is necessary to perform our contract with you, to comply with a legal obligation, for our legitimate business interests, or because you have given consent.

04 / PAYMENTS

Stripe

Ball For Life uses Stripe to process payments.

Payment card information is handled by Stripe through its payment systems. Ball For Life does not need to store your full card number, security code or complete payment-card details in our own website database.

Stripe may collect and process information necessary to complete your payment, carry out fraud prevention, authenticate transactions and comply with financial and legal requirements. Stripe's own privacy terms apply to the information it processes directly.

05 / MARKETING

Email Marketing

If you specifically subscribe to Ball For Life marketing emails, we will use the email address you provide to send information such as new drops, restocks, promotions and brand updates.

Marketing consent is separate from the email address we may need for your account, order, delivery or customer service.

If you create an account or place an order but do not opt in to marketing, we may still keep your email address where it is needed for your account, order records, delivery, support or other legitimate business purposes, but we will not use it to send marketing emails simply because you purchased from us.

You can withdraw your marketing consent at any time by using an unsubscribe option in a marketing email where available, or by contacting us at supportballforlife@gmail.com.

06 / SERVICE PROVIDERS

Who Helps Run The Store

We use third-party service providers to operate Ball For Life. These providers may process personal information on our behalf or directly as part of providing their services.

Supabase

We use Supabase for parts of our backend, including customer account and order information. This can include your name, email address, delivery details and other information needed to manage your account and fulfil your order.

Stripe

We use Stripe to securely process payments and payment-related information.

Cloudflare

We use Cloudflare to host and deliver parts of our website and to help with website performance, availability and security.

We may also share limited information with delivery companies, customer-service providers, professional advisers, authorities or other providers where necessary to fulfil an order, operate the business, protect our rights or comply with the law.

07 / COOKIES & ANALYTICS

Cookies & Store Analytics

Essential browser storage supports your shopping bag, account, security, currency selection and saved privacy choices. We also remember newsletter signup and whether the invitation has appeared during this visit, so we do not keep asking.

Optional store analytics is off until you accept it. If you agree, we use random browser and visit identifiers to count page views, product views, bag actions, checkout visits, payment starts and newsletter signups. We record the referring website's hostname and a broad device category, not its full referral URL. Analytics does not include your name, email, address, form contents or card details, and is not linked to your account.

These activity records are held in our Supabase backend and accessible to authorised store administrators. This is not advertising tracking and we do not currently install Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or TikTok Pixel. Hosting, payment and security providers may process technical information such as IP addresses for their own service functions, as described in their policies.

Your privacy choice expires after 180 days. The analytics browser identifier expires after 90 days; a visit expires after 30 minutes of inactivity or when the browser session ends. Activity older than 90 days is removed when new activity is received. If the store has no new activity, cleanup takes place on the next analytics request.

You can reject analytics and still shop normally. Use here or in the footer to change your mind. Withdrawing stops new collection and removes analytics identifiers from this browser; it does not erase records already received. Newsletter signup is a separate choice, and you can unsubscribe through the emails we send.

08 / DATA RETENTION

How Long We Keep Data

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including to fulfil orders, maintain customer accounts, provide support, prevent fraud, resolve disputes and meet tax, accounting or other legal obligations.

Different types of information may need to be kept for different periods. When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it, anonymise it or otherwise stop using it where appropriate.

09 / INTERNATIONAL PROCESSING

Where Data May Be Processed

Some of the technology providers we use operate internationally. This means personal information may be processed or stored outside the UK.

Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards or the protections provided by our service providers and applicable data-protection law when personal information is transferred internationally.

10 / YOUR RIGHTS

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Request a copy of your personal information.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Ask us to delete personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Ask us to restrict how personal information is used in certain circumstances.
  • Object to certain uses of your personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where our processing is based on consent.
  • Request data portability where that right applies.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a privacy request.

You may also have the right to complain to the UK data-protection regulator if you believe your personal information has been handled unlawfully.

11 / SECURITY

Protecting Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect customer information and use established third-party providers for important parts of the store, including Stripe for payments, Supabase for backend data and Cloudflare for hosting and website delivery.

No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should keep your login details confidential and contact us if you believe your account or personal information has been compromised.

12 / CHANGES

Updates To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, legal obligations or business practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

13 / CONTACT

Privacy Questions

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy, want to exercise a privacy right or want to ask how your information is being used, contact Ball For Life using the email address below.

Privacy & Customer Support supportballforlife@gmail.com
