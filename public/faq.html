FAQ

Everything you need to know about ordering from Ball For Life — from delivery and sizing to payments, returns and product care.

01 / ORDERS

Orders

How do I know if my order has gone through?

After successfully completing checkout, you should receive confirmation that your order has been created. Please make sure the email address used at checkout is correct so you can receive order updates.

Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?

Contact us as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel an order. Once an order has entered processing or has been dispatched, we may not be able to make changes.

This does not affect any rights you may have under applicable consumer law.

I entered the wrong delivery address. What should I do?

Contact us immediately with your order number and the correct address. If the parcel has not yet been dispatched, we will do our best to update it. Once an order has been handed to the courier, an address change may no longer be possible.

Can I order more than one item at a time?

Yes. You can add multiple products, colours and sizes to your bag before checking out.

02 / SHIPPING

Shipping

How long does delivery normally take?

Most orders are expected to arrive within 4–7 working days after dispatch.

In some cases, particularly for overseas or international shipments, delivery can take up to 3 weeks because of international transit, customs or courier delays.

My parcel has not arrived after 3 weeks. What should I do?

Please contact us as soon as possible with your order number so that we can investigate the shipment.

If an order is confirmed as lost or undelivered, we will provide an appropriate resolution, which may include a replacement or full refund, subject to your applicable consumer rights.

For full details, read our Shipping & Returns Policy.

What happens if I wait too long to report a missing parcel?

Please report delivery problems promptly. If you do not contact us within 2 months of the expected delivery date, our ability to investigate the shipment with the courier may be limited.

Do you ship internationally?

Ball For Life may fulfil orders to supported destinations shown during checkout. Delivery times for overseas orders can be longer than domestic deliveries.

03 / RETURNS

Returns

When can I request a return?

Under our store returns policy, a return request may be considered if your item arrives seriously damaged or defective, if you receive the wrong product, or if we send the wrong colour or size compared with your confirmed order.

How long do I have to report an issue?

Please contact us within 7 days of receiving your order and include your order number plus clear photographs or videos showing the problem.

What counts as serious damage?

Potential examples include substantial tears, major holes, unusable components, significant printing defects, severe staining or another fault that materially affects the product.

Small packaging marks, loose threads or minor variations that do not materially affect the product may not be considered serious damage under our store policy.

Where can I read the full return policy?

You can find the full details on our Shipping & Returns page.

04 / PRODUCTS & SIZING

Products & Sizing

How do I choose the right size?

Check the size information shown on the product page before ordering. If a dedicated size guide is available, use the garment measurements rather than relying only on the size name.

Will every colour look exactly the same as it does on my screen?

We aim to show product colours as accurately as possible, but screens and display settings can make colours appear slightly different. Small colour variations can also occur between production batches.

How should I care for my Ball For Life tee?

Always follow the care label supplied with the garment. As a general rule, washing carefully, avoiding excessive heat and keeping printed garments away from harsh treatments will help preserve the fabric and graphic for longer.

Will sold-out products come back?

Some items may be restocked while others may be limited drops. Keep an eye on the website and Ball For Life social channels for release and restock updates.

05 / PAYMENTS

Payments

Which payment methods do you accept?

Available payment methods are shown during checkout. Your site currently displays support for major methods including Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, Apple Pay, Klarna and Discover.

Can I pay in a different currency?

The store supports GBP, USD and EUR display options. The final amount and any conversion applied by your bank or payment provider can depend on the payment method used.

Why was my payment declined?

A payment can be declined for several reasons, including incorrect card details, insufficient funds, bank security checks or payment-provider restrictions. Check your details and try again, or contact your bank or payment provider if the issue continues.

06 / ACCOUNT & DISCOUNTS

Account & Discounts

Do I need an account to shop?

If guest checkout is available at the time you order, you can complete a purchase without creating an account. An account can make it easier to keep your details and order information together.

How do I hear about new drops?

Subscribe to the Ball For Life newsletter and follow the brand on Instagram and TikTok to keep up with new drops, restocks and announcements.

Where do I enter a discount code?

If a promotion or discount code is available, enter it at the relevant stage of checkout. Promotional offers may have their own eligibility requirements and expiry dates.

Still need help?

We're here to sort it.

If your question is about a specific order, include your order number and as much information as possible when contacting Ball For Life.
