# Ball For Life

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Basketball fans, hoopers and streetwear customers shopping primarily on mobile. Store owners use the existing admin pages for orders, customers and creator codes.

## Product Purpose

Sell real Ball For Life clothing with a clear, trustworthy route from browsing to secure payment and order tracking.

## Positioning

An independent basketball-inspired streetwear brand, inspired by the fear of being average.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript deployed to Cloudflare. Preserve the product catalogue, images, variants, prices, currency conversion, cart storage, Supabase authentication, rewards, discounts, visitor analytics, newsletter, order tracking and Stripe integration. Free UK shipping applies to qualifying orders over GBP 65 after discount. Hoodies and tracksuits are coming soon. Do not fabricate product, stock, delivery or review claims.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the BFL logos and authentic photography. The user requests a bold, minimal, athletic, editorial identity inspired by Nike and Represent, with black, off-white, charcoal and neutral grey. Clear mobile layouts and restrained motion take priority.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing campaign photography in public/hero-*.jpg, real product images in public/graphic tee, and customer photos and videos in public/assets/community and public/kaden-review.mp4. Existing policies and customer testimonials remain factual source material.
