## Brand Commitments

Keep the BFL logos and authentic photography. The user requests a bold, minimal, athletic, editorial identity inspired by Nike and Represent, with black, off-white, charcoal and neutral grey. Clear mobile layouts and restrained motion take priority.

The October 2026 fashion refinement keeps the original BFL header logo by explicit user choice. Use the supplied handwritten Ball For Life wordmark for the homepage title and the supplied looping logo once in the newsletter band. Shopping headings use quieter Inter typography; retain condensed campaign story lettering. The goal is premium, fashion-led presentation, not additional promotional claims or animation.

The homepage flows from New Arrivals directly to white-background customer reviews and Shop by Category. The former More Than Basketball photo band is removed at the user's request. Keep all supplied customer media, attributions and correct shop links.
